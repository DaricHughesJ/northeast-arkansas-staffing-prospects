# Northeast Arkansas Staffing Employer Prospects

A standalone prospecting and sales enablement package for a temporary staffing agency serving Northeast Arkansas. This repository is separate from NEA-IT and contains only the staffing-agency package.

## Contents

- `Employer_Prospects_and_Rate_Model.xlsx` — 57 employer candidates, source links, evidence limits, qualification notes, and a blank staffing rate model.
- `Top_15_Employer_Prospects_and_30_Day_Plan.pdf` — first-call targets and a four-week outreach sequence.
- `Staffing_Employer_Outreach_Playbook.pdf` — call, discovery, qualification, objection, and quote-preparation guidance.
- `employer_leads.json` — machine-readable prospect/source data.
- `START_HERE.md` — research scope and how to use the package.
- `PACKAGE_MANIFEST.txt` and `Temporary_Staffing_Employer_Prospecting_Kit.zip` — package inventory and handoff archive.

## Evidence limits

Research checked October 12, 2026. Prospects are accounts worth qualifying, not confirmed staffing buyers. Public job listings, employer size, and expansion announcements do not establish temporary labor demand, agency use, or vendor access. No employer was contacted.

The agency's service mix, insurance, screening, payroll, safety, and placement capabilities have not been verified. Confirm these before offering roles, especially driving, skilled trades, food production, construction, healthcare, and other safety-sensitive work.
