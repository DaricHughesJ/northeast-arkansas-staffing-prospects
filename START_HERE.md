# Northeast Arkansas Staffing Employer Prospecting Kit

**Research checked:** October 12, 2026  
**Audience:** Owner of a temporary staffing agency considering employer accounts in Jonesboro, Paragould, and the surrounding Northeast Arkansas market.

This is a separate staffing-agency package. It does not contain or replace the existing technology-services materials.

## Files

- `Employer_Prospects_and_Rate_Model.xlsx` — 57 deduplicated employer candidates, public signals, local phone/address where verified, role-fit hypotheses, contact roles, individualized opening questions, source register, status tracking, and a blank bill-rate / payroll-cash worksheet.
- `Top_15_Employer_Prospects_and_30_Day_Plan.pdf` — the 15 ranked first-call targets and a manageable four-week outreach sequence.
- `Staffing_Employer_Outreach_Playbook.pdf` — a practical call script, discovery checklist, truthful answers to employer questions, objections, qualification rules, and a pre-quote checklist.
- `employer_leads.json` — the machine-readable source data used to build the spreadsheet and prospect guide.

## What qualifies a prospect here

The research identifies **employers worth qualifying**, not buyers already proven to need staffing. The strongest evidence is a public local hiring signal for production, assembly, machine operation, fabrication, warehousing, logistics, or construction roles. Other accounts are included because a credible local industry directory lists a relevant production/distribution operation or a recent expansion announcement. Those signals justify a focused question; they do not establish that an employer uses a staffing agency, accepts new suppliers, or has a temporary vacancy.

The agency’s service mix is not yet known. This first pass therefore emphasizes production, warehouse, skilled manufacturing, logistics, and construction accounts. It flags specialized trade, driving, food-site, and higher-hazard assignments for capability and safety qualification. If the agency specializes in clinical, education, office, hospitality, or another labor category, re-rank the list after confirming that specialty.

## Use the workbook

1. Start on `Overview`; read the evidence limitations.
2. Filter `Employer Leads` by `Priority` and begin with the 15 ranked accounts. `P1`, `P2`, and `P3` mean research/outreach priority, not purchase likelihood.
3. Before outreach, check whether a listing is still current and whether the agency can fill the role. Ask who handles contingent labor and whether the employer accepts a new staffing supplier.
4. Track calls only after they happen. The current status for every prospect is `Not contacted`.
5. Use `Rate Model` after entering actual per-hour payroll, insurance, benefit, recruiting, administration, target-margin, headcount, hours, and payment-delay inputs. No wage, markup, margin target, or client bill rate has been supplied or assumed.
6. Use `Sources` to check every research basis and refresh public information before acting.

## Research limits

- No employer was contacted, and no forms were submitted.
- Public job listings may be direct-hire-only or stale. The city jobs page says it is regularly updated but does not prove the employer has an agency opening.
- No public source reviewed here confirmed any named employer currently uses an outside staffing agency.
- Some Jonesboro employment figures are from an MSA industry guide and may not represent a single site or current payroll. Paragould employer bands are Greene County estimates.
- Unknown addresses, decision-maker names, phones, supplier rules, job order details, pay rates, employer-of-record model, agency insurance, and screening capabilities are left unknown or stated as questions.
- InnovAsian and SANY Nutrition entries are 2026 announcements, not verified operating sites or current openings.

## First calls

Use the personalized opening question in each prospect row. The call objective is to learn whether a real temporary, seasonal, project, or leave-coverage need exists, who authorizes vendors, and whether the agency has the capability and economics to serve it. Do not present an unverified vacancy as a known staffing problem.

## Compliance reminder

The source register includes Arkansas Act 438, which repealed the Arkansas Private Employment Agency Act and certain related licensing statutes in 2025. That repeal does not resolve every obligation that may apply to the agency. Temporary staffing also raises payroll, tax, insurance, workers’ compensation, safety, wage/hour, contract, and possible joint-employer issues. Confirm the agency’s exact model and obligations with qualified Arkansas counsel, a payroll/tax professional, and its insurance adviser before offering or filling an order.
