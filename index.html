A simple way to use it

The research helps prepare relevant conversations. It does not confirm a current opening or an employer’s interest in agency labor.

Read the top 15

Use the priority guide to choose a small first group and see the proposed 30-day cadence.

Check fit before calling

Confirm the public signal is still current, identify who handles contingent labor, and verify the agency can safely fill the role.

Use the workbook to track

Record only real outreach and enter actual payroll, insurance, recruiting, and payment-delay inputs before using the rate model.

What this research does and does not say

The 57 companies are employer candidates worth qualifying. Public hiring signals, employer size, and expansion announcements do not prove temporary labor demand, agency use, supplier access, or purchase intent. No employer has been contacted.

The agency’s service mix, insurance, screening, payroll, and safety capabilities were not verified. Confirm the agency’s fit and applicable requirements before discussing or accepting an assignment, especially in driving, skilled trades, food production, construction, healthcare, and other safety-sensitive work.

Research scope: public sources checked October 12, 2026. Contact details, job listings, site status, and vendor processes can change; verify them before outreach. The rate model intentionally contains no assumed pay rate, markup, margin target, or client bill rate.
